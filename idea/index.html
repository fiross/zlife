1. The Problem

Over 5 million neurodivergent children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, and Sensory Processing Differences experience severe daily dysregulation. Traditional care tracking is broken:

  • Information Chasm: Home sleep and medication states are completely invisible to classroom educators when the morning bell rings.
  • Misunderstood Signals: Severe sensory distress (ear covering, pacing, non-verbal shutdown) is misdiagnosed as behavioral defiance.
  • Caregiver Burnout: Exhausted parents lack cognitive capacity to manually detect multi-week correlations across diet, sleep, and hormonal cycles.

2. The Z-Life Solution

A fast, tactile dual-role application designed specifically for neurodivergent individuals (initially built and validated for Zeba, a 15-year-old student):

  • Dual-Role Authentication: Fast 4-digit keypad separating Parent full tracking (PIN 1234) from Teacher instant safety glance and Sensory SOS (PIN 5678).
  • 10 High-Impact Clinical Vectors: Sleep duration (steppers/chips), dynamic medications, emotional states, cycle/menstruation tracking, sensory behaviors, calming supports, enrichment activities, food types, and narrative voice notes.
  • Zero-Friction Adoption: Under 60 seconds per day logging with zero full-page reloads.

3. The Core Unfair Advantage: Google Gemini LLM Engine

Instead of leaving logs as dead data, Z-Life pipes structured daily signals directly into Google Gemini:

  • Predictive Meltdown Radar: Forecasts high-risk overload time windows (e.g., loud cafeteria transitions following <6h sleep).
  • Personalized Calming Prescriptions: Recommends individualized sensory diet interventions (joint compression, Walker's earmuffs, oral sensory gum) before distress escalates.
  • Automated Teacher Morning Handoff: Delivers a concise 3-bullet morning briefing so educators know exactly what accommodations are needed today.

4. The Non-Profit Vision: Zero Business Monetization

Unlike commercial startups seeking venture returns, Z-Life is strictly a non-profit social good platform:

  • 100% Free for Needy Families: No subscription tiers, no paywalls, and no feature gating for vulnerable caregivers.
  • Zero Commercial Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or commercialize sensitive neurodivergent health or behavioral data.
  • Sustainability: Funded and sustained solely via Google Cloud AI for Social Good grants, accessibility research fellowships, and philanthropic donors.

5. What We Are Requesting from Google

  • Google Cloud & Vertex AI credits to scale Gemini multimodal inference and context caching for families in need.
  • Mentorship from Google Health, Google Accessibility, and Google.org social impact teams.
  • Integration support for Google Classroom and Google Workspace for Education.
